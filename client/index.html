Current Top Quarterbacks

A running list of the NFL's top-performing quarterbacks this season, tracked here for quick reference and comparison.

1. Patrick Mahomes

Position: Quarterback — Team: Kansas City Chiefs

Known for his elite arm talent and improvisational playmaking, Mahomes has quarterbacked the Chiefs to multiple Super Bowl championships since taking over as starter in 2018.

Career passer rating: 97.5

2. Josh Allen

Position: Quarterback — Team: Buffalo Bills

A powerful dual-threat playmaker, Allen pairs a cannon arm with the athleticism to extend plays and pick up yards with his legs, making him one of the most complete quarterbacks in the league.

Career passer rating: 92.6

3. Lamar Jackson

Position: Quarterback — Team: Baltimore Ravens

One of the most explosive running quarterbacks in NFL history, Jackson has earned MVP recognition for his blend of blazing speed and increasingly precise passing.

Career passer rating: 102.7
